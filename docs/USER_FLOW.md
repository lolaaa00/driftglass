# Reviewer path

1. Connect an injected wallet and switch to Studionet `61999`.
2. Open **Compose**, enter the subject, canonical domain, beneficiary, named right, official HTTPS sources, precise reliance clauses, interval, and note.
3. Copy the generated authority manifest to `https://<canonical-domain>/.well-known/driftglass.json`; its policy digest binds the complete proposal.
4. Create the draft and wait for finalized majority agreement, successful leader execution, and the contract reread.
5. Open the record and activate the baseline in a separate consensus transaction. Activation must fail if the domain manifest is absent or mismatched.
6. Inspect authority verification, verified excerpts, full-response commitments, coverage metadata, and the `ENFORCEABLE` right state.
7. As the bound beneficiary, enter an action description and exercise the right. Confirm an immutable action-digest receipt appears.
8. Open **Checkpoint** from an active record. Any connected account may initiate an eligible semantic check.
9. Confirm the reread shows a new sequence, clause verdicts, requester, timestamp, aggregate assessment, and the resulting right state.

Failed consensus, ambiguous execution responses, rejected signatures, invalid authority, missing or oversized sources, and inconclusive evidence remain explicit and never display as successful state changes. A `BROKEN` result revokes the right; unavailable, inconclusive, stale, revised, or archived records cannot be exercised.
